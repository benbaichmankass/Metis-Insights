## 0. The reframe — read this before the lanes

The instinct from the roadmap alone is "which strategy or model do we improve
next". Measured against the live system on 2026-08-14, that is the wrong
question, and the numbers say so unambiguously.

### 0.2 What that surface earned

Lifetime real-money `totalPnL` is **−$67.38** at a 26.5% win rate.

**State the population**, per the standing rule: those figures are real-money
only, and `pnlCoverage` on the 30d window is **0.889** (24 MEASURED / 2

One caveat that cuts the other way: 30d `expectancyR` reads **+0.836**, which
looks healthy. It is carried almost entirely by `ict_scalp_5m` at +35.4R over

### 0.3 The `vwap` line is history, not a live problem

`/api/bot/strategy/attribution` shows `vwap` at **318 closed trades, 23.6% win
rate, −$50.71** — by far the largest single real-money contributor, and ~75% of
all real-money closed trades ever. **It is already dead**: `config/strategies.yaml`
has `enabled: false` + `execution: shadow` (the M7 Tier-3 kill, 2026-06-09).
Verified in the field, not inferred from the changelog.

So excluding `vwap`, the *live* fleet's real-money lifetime is roughly
**−$16.67** across ~62 trades. Small, negative, and — critically — **thin**.

### 0.4 The conclusion the numbers force

The binding constraint on this system making money is **not** strategy quality,
model quality, or exit timing. It is that **almost nothing is live, the one live
thing is $280, and several declared-live legs cannot place an order at all**.

An enormous, genuinely rigorous research apparatus (M20–M36) is producing
findings — most of them honest negatives, which is a credit to the process — and
measuring them against a book that cannot act on them. Meanwhile the health
backlog carries 180 open items, of which several are *"this declared-live leg
places nothing"*.

**Therefore the next work is capability integrity, not more research.** Research
lanes continue, but behind it.

silence** check measures `*_eval` events — a leg that evaluates, signals, and has

answers. So "declared live, signals, places NOTHING" falls between them.
