## 2. The single biggest blocker, and why it is structural

**`bracket_geometry :: blocked:no_free_lane_candle_feed` — 25 cells.** That is **36% of
everything remaining** and **51% of everything blocked**. `bracket_geometry` overall is 37 of
the 69 remaining cells; with `exit_head_ml` (16) the two levers are **76.8%** of the remainder.

⚠️ **This is not "nobody wrote the fetcher yet."** The free lane is
**`data.binance.vision`** (`docs/research/e35-rr-floor-walkforward-2026-08-20.md`), a Binance
**crypto** archive, and `scripts/ops/fetch_backtest_candles.py` offers exactly two sources —
`bybit` and `binance_vision`. Both are crypto. All 25 blocked legs are **non-crypto**, with
**zero** crypto among them:

- **IBKR futures (5):** MES ×1, MGC ×3, MHG ×1
- **US equities / ETFs (18):** SPY ×2, QQQ ×2, GLD ×2, TLT ×2, SLV ×2, TQQQ, QLD, IWM, SPLG, SCHA, IEF, GDX, IAUM, USO
- **FX / metal (1):** XAUUSD
- Timeframes: mostly `1d` and `1h`, plus `ict_scalp_mgc_15m` at 15m.

So the block is a **source-coverage** fact, not a backlog item anyone forgot.

## 3. The other M20 thread: M31 P5, and what it says about scheduling

M20's roadmap tail names its blocker as **M31 P5 precondition 2b — live final-MFE, n=1,
"a soak-depth problem no candle data touches."** The proposal
(`docs/design/m31-p5-telemetry-reading-lever-PROPOSAL.md` § 5) already separates the work
from the waiting, and its own instruction is easy to miss:

- **Waiting:** 2b, live soak depth. *"Nothing accelerates it."*
- **Work:** 2a's committed artifact and 3b's walk-forward — and *"both need the SAME
  trainer-side capped sweep at the live timeframes. They should share one sweep rather than
  being scheduled apart."*
- **Done:** 1 (terminal stamp), 3a (the `--rr-floor` lever exists and is instrumented), 2a's mechanism.

**Note the convergence:** 3b's sweep needs *per-leg candles at the live timeframes*
(§ 5 precondition 3b), which is the **same** gap as § 2 above. One feed unblocks both the 25
`bracket_geometry` cells and 3b.

## 4. Soak QUALITY, not just depth — why the workplan's exit items are on this path

2b is a **depth** problem. Two open workplan items are **quality** problems on the same rows,
and depth accrued under them is depth M20 cannot use:

- **Item 1.8** (`BL-20260822-EXIT-REASON-FROZEN-WHEN-PRICE-ARRIVES-LATE`) — of the
  `reconciler_filled` closes where broker truth can adjudicate, **91 of 155 (58.7%)** actually
  reached a declared bracket level. The recorded exit *mechanism* is wrong on the majority of
  adjudicable rows, and every M20 exit-mechanism reading is taken from that field.
- **G.1** (`BL-20260822-FILLS-MATCHER-QTY-GATE-BLOCKS-NETTED-ACCOUNTS`) — `bybit_1`'s netted
  closes are rejected by a 5% qty tolerance at a measured 31–44% overshoot, which is why
  item 1.1 was measurable on only **24.1%** of the main path.

Neither is a detour from M20. They are the reason its evidence base is thin *in quality*
while 2b is thin *in quantity*.
