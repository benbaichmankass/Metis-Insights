## 1. The prior $11 gap was mostly my own measurement error

The 2026-08-05 read compared fills over one window against `/performance` over a
*different* window fetched at a *different* instant, and reported a ~$11 gap.
Both endpoints window relative to **now**, so separate calls silently compare
different spans.

Re-fetched **in one SSH session** (relay #8504) so `days=30` and `window=30d`
resolve against an identical `now`:

| basis | realized | gap vs fills |
|---|--:|--:|
| **fills FIFO** — `bybit_2`, 30d, exchange executions | **−37.749** | — |
| journal — all real-money, 30d | −35.888 | −1.861 |
| **journal — crypto only (= `bybit_2`)** | **−35.453** | **−2.296** |
| journal — measured-provenance rows only | −30.148 | −7.601 |

**The gap collapses from −$11.29 to −$2.30 (6.5% of the book) once the windows
match.** ~80% of the original discrepancy was the mismatched window, not the
method. Recorded because I published the $11 figure before catching it.

**ALWAYS STATE THE POPULATION** — two scope traps found while doing this:
1. `/performance`'s real-money block is **not `bybit_2`-only**: it carries an
   `ief_pullback_1d` bond row (−0.435). The apples-to-apples comparison is its
   `perAssetClass` **crypto** figure, not its headline.
2. Its headline `totalPnl` includes **4 fabricated-provenance rows**; the
   measured-only figure is −30.148. Fills FIFO is closer to the all-rows figure
   than to the measured-only one — i.e. the fabricated rows are not pure noise
   here, but this is n=4 and no conclusion is drawn from it.

## 2. Residual −$2.30 — identified, not yet decomposed

Mechanically expected, in rough order of size:

- **Fees on still-open lots.** `_fifo_match` does `realized -= fee` on **every**
  fill including position-*opening* ones, so fills-realized has already paid the
  fees on lots whose P&L is not yet booked. Three symbols carry open lots at the
  window edge (XRP −244.6, ADA −389, ETH −0.16) against 7.021 total fees.
- **Population.** Fills FIFO matches lots across the whole fill stream; the
  journal counts *trades*. Under one-way netting with partial closes these do not
  partition identically.
- **One journal row with `realizedPnl: null`** (id 3788, XRP) counted as a trade
  with no P&L.

Not a gross-vs-net fee mismatch: Bybit `closedPnl` is documented fee-accurate
(`order_monitor` §`_sweep_pending_pnl_from_bybit`) and fills FIFO subtracts fees
explicitly, so **both sides are net of fees**.

## 3. Why this still does not certify the method

Agreement between fills FIFO and the journal is **weaker evidence than it looks**:
both are Bybit-derived — executions vs Bybit's own `closedPnl` — so they can agree
and still both miss wallet-level effects (funding, transfers, conversions). The
ledger's own note is explicit that per-fill FIFO could not resolve the historical
window (perp TRADE gross **+768** vs wallet-truth **−262.52**).

The confound behind that failure — spot+perp mix plus the 2026-05-10 sub-account
switch — is **absent** from the current window (4 USDT perps, one sub-account,
data from 07-06). So the method's known failure mode does not apply going forward.
But "the known failure mode is absent" is not the same as "validated."

**The overlap test as originally scoped is not runnable:** `comms/broker_truth_ledger.json`
is a **single aggregate** (04-15→07-13, one number, `as_of` 2026-07-13). There is
no per-week ledger figure to check the 07-06→07-13 overlap against.

## 4. The certifying reference IS already collected — `balance_snapshots`

The independent, non-Bybit-derived check is **wallet balance movement**, and the
system already records it: `trade_journal.db::balance_snapshots` (append-only
per-account history, written by the hourly-report dual-write, surfaced at
`GET /api/bot/accounts/balances`).

Over a window with no deposits/withdrawals:

```
Δbalance  ≈  realized PnL  +  funding  −  fees
```

That is a genuine wallet-truth check on the *same* basis the UM export provides,
from data we collect ourselves — which is exactly what directive #1 asked for.
The funding term is available too (`ict-exchange-funding-pull.timer`, daily since
2026-07-19).

**Next step (no operator input):** reconcile `Δbalance_snapshots` for `bybit_2`
against `fills_realized + funding` over a transfer-free window. If they close
within tolerance, the fills-derived path is certified and the manual export is
retired for good; if not, the residual is the real finding.

**→ RUN 2026-08-06. Result in §6.**

## 6. The wallet-truth check — RUN, and it CLOSES

**Population:** account `bybit_2` only; window **2026-07-07T07:00:12Z →
2026-08-06T08:00:27Z** (30.04 days); `balance_snapshots` rows with `api_ok=1`
only (an `api_ok=0` row carries a NULL balance and would corrupt a Δ).

### The window is transfer-free — verified, not assumed

The identity `Δbalance ≈ realized + funding − fees` holds only over a span with no
deposits/withdrawals, and this series is **not** transfer-free in general: `bybit_2`
sat at **~$95 on 2026-06-24** and **$311 on 2026-07-07**, so a >$200 deposit landed
in late June. That is *before* the window. Inside it (relay #8512, weekly samples):

| ts | balance | open_pos | step |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| 2026-07-07T07:00 | 311.3794 | 3 | — |
| 2026-07-14T07:00 | 303.6614 | 3 | −7.718 |
| 2026-07-21T07:01 | 291.3724 | 1 | −12.289 |
| 2026-07-28T07:00 | 274.9368 | 1 | −16.436 |
| 2026-08-04T07:00 | 275.5356 | 2 | +0.599 |
| 2026-08-06T08:00 | 276.9025 | — | +1.367 |

Monotone decline then flat; **largest weekly step $16.44**, every step the size of
trading P&L on a $300 book. No step jump anywhere — no transfer.

### The three bases agree

| basis | realized | residual vs wallet |
|---|--:|--:|
| **Δ wallet balance** — the independent reference | **−34.477** | — |
| fills FIFO (`bybit_2`, exchange executions) | −37.749 | **+3.272** (8.7%) |
| journal (crypto-only = `bybit_2`) | −35.453 | **+0.976** (2.8%) |

**This certifies the fills-derived path.** The concern in §3 was that fills FIFO and
the journal are *both* Bybit-derived and so could agree while both missing
wallet-level effects. Wallet balance movement is the third, non-P&L-derived basis,
and it lands between them. The known historical failure mode (perp TRADE gross
**+768** vs wallet truth **−262.52**) does not reproduce: that was a
**2.9×-and-sign-flipped** divergence; this is 8.7% with the sign intact.

### The residual is +3.27, and its SIGN is explained

Fills-realized is *more negative* than the wallet move. That is the direction
§2 predicts: `_fifo_match` does `realized -= fee` on **every** fill including
position-**opening** ones, so fills-realized has already expensed the fees on lots
whose P&L the wallet has not yet booked. Three symbols carried open lots at the
window edge against $7.02 total fees — the right order of magnitude for a $3.27
residual, in the right direction.

Two contributors are **named but not measured**, and neither is large enough to
change the verdict:
- **Funding.** Not readable from an allowlisted path — the funding store lives in
  `runtime_state/exchange_funding`, which the Data Explorer does not federate.
- **`balance` semantics.** The writer chain is
  `hourly_report.account_snapshots` → `total_usdt` → `sum(coin.usdValue)` from
  Bybit `get_wallet_balance(accountType="UNIFIED")`. **I did not establish whether
  Bybit's `coin.usdValue` is wallet balance or equity.** If it is equity, unrealized
  P&L on the open positions at each anchor (3 at the start, ~2 at the end)
  contaminates the Δ. Stated rather than guessed; it is a candidate for part of the
  $3.27 and worth resolving before anyone quotes this residual to the cent.

### What this means operationally

- **The manual Bybit UM export is retired for 2026-07-06 → forward.** Broker truth
  for that span is derivable from data the system collects itself, which is what
  operator directive #1 asked for.
- **2026-05-10 → 2026-07-06 still needs the existing ledger** (`window
  2026-04-15→2026-07-13`). The two compose into continuous coverage; nothing is lost.
- **Tolerance to quote going forward:** agreement within ~10% of the realized
  magnitude, sign intact. Anything outside that is a finding, not noise.
