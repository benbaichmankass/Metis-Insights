### 4a. The two "why doesn't it survive" answers — and what to do

Both are the **same failure shape**, and in neither case is the strategy the
problem. **Neither needs a system override:** one needs a sizing fix, the other a
config choice already proven offline.

**GLD on `alpaca_portfolio`** (`BL-20260803-GLD-ALPACA-PORTFOLIO-SURVIVAL-SKIP`)
— fails on **position sizing, not edge**. The corrected-cost (0-bps) compat gate
(#8425, faithful, 123 trades) scores the leg at **end_return_mean +32.7%**, a
*higher* return than accounts that PASS. What fails is survival **0.871** (floor
0.90) and P(breach) **0.132** (cap 0.10), and the driver is named in the row:
`alpaca_portfolio` runs **2% risk_pct against its own 5% max_dd / daily caps**.
The identical strategy passes on the ROUTE accounts (survival 0.922) purely
because they size lower. → **Tweak the sizing, don't override the gate.** Action =
option (b): a per-strategy risk override for the gld leg on `alpaca_portfolio`,
re-run `account_compat_matrix`, route only if it clears. Overriding instead would
produce exactly the "paper book carrying a strategy its own ruleset would reject"
state the row exists to flag. *(The confirmed `trending {short: off}` cell is
independent and unaffected.)*

**ict_scalp-on-gold** (`PB-20260728-ICTSCALP-GOLD-GATE-CONFIG`) — the *strategy*
survives; the **fitted confidence gate** is what doesn't. The deep 4-fold
walk-forward (Dukascopy spot XAU 15m, 2012→now, MGC economics) replicates the
**ungated** edge at **+41.09R, 3/4 folds** and `conf070_fixed` at +21.8R, while
the **fitted** gate collapses to **+7.94R** and swings ~28R between two equally
defensible splits — derive-window-fragile, discarding most of the edge. Two
independent splits agree: **ungated ≥ conf070 ≫ fitted gate.** → **Ship the
ungated (or `conf070_fixed`) config; never the fitted gate.** The offline question
is *answered* — no further validation is owed. Action = wire an ict_scalp-gold leg
to `ib_paper` at `execution: live` as a **mechanics-only paper soak** per the
`new-strategy` skill, with tempered expectations recorded up front (exp-R
**+0.094**, not the earlier +0.197; fold-1-type negative regimes — the 2014–17
fold ran −14.08R baseline — are expected, not a failure signal).

**Shared lesson worth keeping:** in both cases the *guardrail* (survival gate /
fitted confidence gate) is what failed, and the right response was to fix the
sizing or the config choice — **not** to override the guardrail. That is the
opposite of the reflex, and it is the pattern to apply to the next one of these.
