MAE_over_D >= 1.25  =>  price would ALSO have reached a 1.25x wider stop
                        (pre-e35 atr_stop_mult 2.5 vs the shipped 2.0).
MFE_over_D          =>  how far the trade EVER went in its favour, in units of its own risk.
