## Building practices — the stopping rules

These bind every session, not just Phase 0.

### Verification is proportional to blast radius

| the change touches | what it must carry |
|---|---|
| **live order path · money-DB writes · risk/sizing · account mode** | falsification, a live positive control, the population stated. No shortcut. |
| **runtime observability that can refuse or resize a trade** | a test shown to fire on a synthetic positive |
| **notifications · docs · CI wiring · research tooling** | a unit test shown to fire. **Stop there.** |

**The rule that was missing: stop at the level where a wrong answer costs money.**
A swallowed Telegram is worth a unit test, not two real operator pings. If you are
verifying the verifier, you have gone one level too far — unless the thing being
verified can move money.

### One copy of every fact

A fact lives in exactly one place and is **derived**, never restated. On
2026-08-21 three "canonical" documents gave the required-check list as **15, 9 and
3**; only the last was true. Prefer reading branch protection, the config, or the
code over mirroring it.

### One session at a time

Serial unless the work is provably disjoint. Three concurrent sessions spent a
visible fraction of their effort on merge slots, board tail proofs and shared-file
three-way reconciliations. `session-coordination` still governs when concurrency
is genuinely warranted.

### Open PRs non-draft

Flipping draft→ready **after** CI passes re-runs every required check on the same
commit and leaves two same-SHA context sets, so `get_check_runs` can no longer be
read by name and conclusion alone — you must compare `started_at`. Cost ~12
minutes on #10109.
