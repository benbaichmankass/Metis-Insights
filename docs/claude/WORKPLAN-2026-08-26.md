## Why this replaces the 08-21 plan

Operator directive, 2026-08-26: *"I wanna make sure that we have a coherent work
plan based on what actually exists, not what we think exists or what was supposed
to exist, but doesn't."*

The 08-21 plan was rewritten around this table:

| what | it said |
|---|---|
| closes via a **declared bracket** | **30 of 369 — 8.1%** |
| closes `reconciler_filled` | 148 — 40% |

⚠️ **THAT TABLE IS COMPUTED FROM `exit_reason`, AND `exit_reason` IS WRONG ABOUT
EXACTLY THIS.** Measured 2026-08-26 on `ict_scalp_5m` / `bybit_2` / closed rows
with exit_price + stop_loss + take_profit_1 present, n=28: **22 of 28 (78.6%)
exited within 0.05% of price of a declared bracket level**, and **11 of the 15
rows labelled `reconciler_filled` were among them** (4 at the stop, 7 at the
target). Stable across a 0.02–0.50% tolerance sweep (20/22/22/24/26/28), so it is
not a cutoff artifact.

The brackets fire. The *attribution* goes to the reconciler. So a plan built on
"the declared exits are not happening" was built on a label, not a measurement —
and it pointed work at an exit path that largely works.

**This is not a small correction to one number.** `/api/bot/performance` publishes
`perExitPath` specifically to cut coverage *by exit path*; `CLAUDE.md` records six
paths at 0.0 coverage read off this same field; and the structural-health reading
that *"64.7% of closes come from cleanup machinery"* is a claim about **mechanism**
sourced from a label now shown wrong for the majority of rows it is applied to.
Every one of those inherits the defect.

**Hence this plan's ordering principle: fix the instruments before acting on what
they read.** GATE 0 below is not overhead — it is the precondition for every other
lane being scored honestly, and the operator has since elevated it to the number-one
blocker in its own words.

### Why deep research before building is the right sequencing

The operator raised this as a possibility; the evidence supports it as the
default. Facts 1–3 are all instrument defects, not strategy defects: the exit
label is wrong, the PnL labels are 74% non-measured, and the one scalar two
milestones are built on has been measured at 0.522 against a 0.55 gate. **Building
on top of that produces confident work whose foundation nobody has checked** —
which is precisely the pattern that cost this session seven corrections and the
08-21 plan its premise.
