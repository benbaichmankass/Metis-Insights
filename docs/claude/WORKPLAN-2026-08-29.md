## ⚠️ A CORRECTION TO MY OWN WORK THIS SESSION — read before using Lane B

Earlier today I measured `target_extension_soak` at **900 rows / 21 legs / 6 days,
900 of 900 `sentinel_no_expectation`**, and reported that *"the target-extension
lever cannot fire anywhere in the fleet"* and that the extend-the-winner half of

**That over-reads the evidence, and B3 in the 08-26 plan already established why.**
`evaluate_extension` returns `EXT_NO_EXPECTATION` **before** the approach gate, and
`not_approaching` is excluded from `_LOGGED_STATES`. So sentinel legs log on every
evaluation while real-target legs log **nothing at all** until price approaches
their target. An all-sentinel soak is therefore the **expected composition**, not a
finding about the lever.

**What the soak can and cannot say:**

| claim | supported? |
|---|---|
| The 4 real-target legs contributed zero rows in the window | ✅ yes |
| No real-target trade approached its target in the window | ✅ that is what zero rows means |
| The lever would never fire | ❌ **not distinguishable** — opposite follow-ups |

My reading reproduced the 08-26 composition on a fresher window (900 rows to their
n=856) and added nothing. Recorded because the mistake is instructive: I measured a
real thing, and then attached a conclusion the instrument cannot support.

**What IS independently true**, from `config/strategies.yaml` rather than the soak —
this part stands and I verified it this session:

| `tp_r` on the 52 **enabled** strategies | count |
|---|---|

The only non-prop live leg carrying a real target is **`xrp_pullback_2h`** (`tp_r: 3.0`).
So the population on which an extension lever could ever act is genuinely tiny. That
is a statement about **declared geometry**, not about the lever's behaviour, and the
