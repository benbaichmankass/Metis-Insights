## The two questions the brief asked

- **pipeline.py's refusal rules.** `validate()` (pipeline.py:261-348) refuses a record that is missing any of: `due_when` (with `due_date` or `clears_when`), `origin.ref`, `origin.rerun`, or `terminal_reason` on a done/killed record. It also refuses `terminal_reason` on an open record and `routed` without `routed_to`. `append()` also enforces `intent` new/update plus an identity check. `--self-test` passes, `tests/test_pipeline.py` and `tests/test_render_daily_brief.py` pass (37 tests), and `--check` on the live store reads 1708 records → 1395 items, 0 unreadable, 0 invalid, 3 grandfathered collisions, 97 due, 72 unrouted. The rules do what they say, but they check form only: a rerun that merely prints an archive row passes, and a `routed` item whose owner has finished stays routed (two medium findings above).
- **Is `render_section_0` called by anything that runs?** **Yes. I observed it, not just inferred it from code:** `GET /api/bot/work/brief` (src/web/api/routers/work.py:2150-2213) → `render_daily_brief.build()` → `pipeline.render_section_0`. A live curl at 2026-09-27T13:29Z returned `present: true`, 97 due and 72 unrouted, which matches my independent local `--check` exactly. **Not settled:** whether the SPA Workflow page (the `ict-trader-dashboard` repo, outside this lane) actually displays `/brief`.
